# Roadmap

polito-mcp currently wraps **15 tools** across the most-used parts of the student
portal. The PoliTO API exposes roughly **71 endpoints**, so there's a clear path toward
full coverage. Priorities below are ordered by student impact.

## High priority

| Area | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Exam booking** | Booking and cancelling sessions is directly actionable — the biggest UX win. |
| **Course files & videos** | Slides and recorded lectures are used daily. |
| **Announcements** | Fills a gap students actively miss today. |

## Medium priority

| Area | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Free-room finder** | Unique — no easy alternative exists for finding open study rooms. |
| **Job offers** | Career-relevant for every student. |
| **People search** | Fast way to find a professor's contact or office. |
| **Course statistics** | Pass rates and grade distributions help plan future courses. |

## Nice to have

| Area | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Support tickets** | Completes the secretariat experience end to end. |
| **News & surveys** | Low effort, rounds out coverage. |
| **ESC & service links** | Direct Moodle/webmail jumps and European Student Card. |

## Beyond endpoints

A few ideas that combine multiple tools into something greater:

- **Smart morning brief** — today's lectures, approaching deadlines and unread messages in one answer.
- **Provisional-grade advisor** — accept-vs-reject analysis with GPA impact.
- **Graduation optimizer** — fastest path to degree given current credits and grades.
- **Calendar export** — lectures and deadlines as a subscribable iCal feed.

Want to influence the order, or build one of these? The project is open source —
[open an issue or PR](https://github.com/FAZIO11/polito-mcp-app).
